Fudgy Espresso Dark Brownies

Dense, glossy chocolate squares with a crackly top crust and a subtle espresso depth.

Prep Time: 15 mins Cook Time: 32 mins Yield: 12 squares

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup (170g) unsalted butter
  • 200g (7 oz) dark chocolate (70% cacao), chopped
  • 1 1/4 cups (250g) granulated sugar
  • 3 large eggs, room temperature
  • 1 1/2 tsp espresso powder + 1 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 3/4 cup (95g) all-purpose flour + 1/3 cup (30g) unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 175°C (350°F) and line an 8x8-inch pan with parchment paper.
  2. Melt butter and chopped dark chocolate gently over low heat or a bain-marie, then stir until smooth.
  3. Whisk sugar, eggs, espresso powder, and vanilla vigorously for 2 minutes until pale and thick.
  4. Gently fold in the melted chocolate, followed by flour, cocoa powder, and salt until just combined.
  5. Bake for 30 to 34 minutes until a toothpick comes out with moist fudgy crumbs, then cool completely before slicing.
Chef's Note: Chill the pan in the refrigerator for 30 minutes before cutting for ultra-clean bakery edges.
