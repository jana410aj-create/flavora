Brown Butter Chocolate Chip Cookies

Nutty browned butter, deep dark chocolate chunks, and crisp golden edges with a tender center.

Prep Time: 15 mins Cook Time: 11 mins Yield: 16 cookies

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (226g) unsalted butter, browned and slightly cooled
  • 3/4 cup (150g) packed dark brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup (100g) granulated white sugar
  • 1 large egg plus 1 egg yolk, room temperature
  • 2 tsp pure vanilla extract
  • 2 cups (250g) all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda + 3/4 tsp fine sea salt
  • 1 1/2 cups (250g) chopped bittersweet chocolate

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F) and line two baking sheets with parchment paper.
  2. Whisk the browned butter with both sugars until glossy and well combined.
  3. Beat in the egg, egg yolk, and vanilla extract until smooth.
  4. Fold in the flour, baking soda, and sea salt just until no dry streaks remain, then stir in the chocolate chunks.
  5. Scoop tablespoon-sized mounds onto the sheets and bake 10 to 12 minutes until edges are golden while centers stay soft.
Chef's Note: Sprinkle flaky sea salt right as the warm cookies leave the oven to balance the caramel notes of browned butter.
