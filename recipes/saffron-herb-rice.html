Saffron & Pistachio Basmati Pilaf

Fragrant long-grain basmati rice steeped with golden saffron threads, toasted pistachios, and barberries.

Prep Time: 12 mins Cook Time: 20 mins Yield: 4 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups long-grain basmati rice, rinsed and soaked 20 mins
  • 1/4 tsp saffron threads steeped in 3 tbsp warm water
  • 2 1/2 cups vegetable or chicken broth
  • 2 tbsp ghee or unsalted butter
  • 1/3 cup raw shelled pistachios, roughly chopped
  • 1/4 cup dried barberries or tart cranberries + cinnamon pinch

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Melt ghee in a medium pot over medium heat and lightly toast chopped pistachios for 1 minute; set aside half for garnish.
  2. Drain soaked rice and stir it into the pot with a pinch of cinnamon and salt for 1 minute.
  3. Pour in warm broth, bring to a boil, then cover and simmer on low for 12 minutes until liquid is absorbed.
  4. Drizzle the steeped saffron water over the top of the rice, cover again, and rest 5 minutes off heat.
  5. Fluff gently with a fork, fold in barberries and pistachios, and serve warm.
Chef's Note: Steeping saffron in warm water first unlocks its aroma and vibrant golden hue evenly across the rice.
