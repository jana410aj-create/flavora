Honeyed Citrus & Berry Galette

Rustic folded pastry tart filled with bright orange-zest cream, berries, and wildflower honey glaze.

Prep Time: 20 mins Cook Time: 35 mins Yield: 6 slices

Ingredients

  • 1 sheet chilled shortcrust or puff pastry dough
  • 1/2 cup mascarpone or thick cream cheese + 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp grated orange zest
  • 2 cups mixed berries (raspberries, blueberries, or sliced strawberries)
  • 1 tbsp beaten egg for brushing crust + 1 tbsp honey for drizzling

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 190°C (375°F) and place the pastry sheet on a parchment-lined baking tray.
  2. Beat mascarpone, honey, and orange zest until smooth, then spread in the center leaving a 2-inch border.
  3. Arrange the mixed berries over the cream layer and fold the outer pastry edges up and over the filling.
  4. Brush the folded crust with beaten egg and bake for 32 to 35 minutes until deep golden brown.
  5. Drizzle warm honey over the galette right after baking and cool slightly before slicing.
Chef's Note: Keeping the pastry chilled until it enters the hot oven helps the folded crust puff and stay flaky.
