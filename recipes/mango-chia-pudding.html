Coconut & Cardamom Mango Chia Bowl

Chilled coconut-cardamom chia pudding layered with ripe mango puree and toasted coconut flakes.

Prep Time: 10 mins Cook Time: 0 mins Yield: 2 servings

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup chia seeds
  • 1 1/2 cups canned full-fat coconut milk
  • 1 1/2 tbsp maple syrup or honey
  • 1/4 tsp ground cardamom + pinch of salt
  • 1 ripe mango, diced (half blended smooth, half cubed)
  • 2 tbsp toasted coconut flakes for topping

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Whisk chia seeds, coconut milk, maple syrup, cardamom, and salt in a bowl or jar.
  2. Let sit for 5 minutes and whisk again so the seeds do not clump at the bottom.
  3. Cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours (or overnight) until thick and pudding-like.
  4. Spoon mango puree into serving bowls, top with the chilled chia pudding, and add fresh mango cubes.
  5. Finish with toasted coconut flakes just before serving.
Chef's Note: Stirring twice during the first 15 minutes guarantees an even texture throughout every spoonful.
