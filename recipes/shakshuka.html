Spiced Tomato & Pepper Shakshuka

Poached eggs nestled in a smoky roasted-pepper and cumin tomato sauce served with crusty bread.

Prep Time: 10 mins Cook Time: 22 mins Yield: 4 servings

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 medium yellow onion, finely diced
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 1/2 tsp ground cumin + 1 tsp smoked paprika + 1/4 tsp cayenne
  • 1 can (400g) crushed tomatoes
  • 6 large eggs
  • Fresh cilantro or parsley and crumbled feta for serving

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil in a wide skillet over medium heat and sauté onion and red bell pepper for 6 minutes until softened.
  2. Add garlic, cumin, smoked paprika, and cayenne; cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
  3. Pour in crushed tomatoes, season with salt, and simmer uncovered for 10 to 12 minutes until thickened.
  4. Make 6 small wells in the sauce, crack an egg into each, cover with a lid, and cook 5 to 7 minutes until whites set and yolks stay runny.
  5. Top with herbs and feta, then bring the skillet straight to the table with warm bread.
Chef's Note: Keep the heat on a gentle simmer once the eggs go in so the yolks poach evenly without toughening.
