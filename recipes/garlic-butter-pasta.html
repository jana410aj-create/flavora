Roasted Garlic & Basil Linguine

Silky linguine tossed in sweet confit-style garlic oil, blistered cherry tomatoes, and fresh basil.

Prep Time: 10 mins Cook Time: 15 mins Yield: 4 servings

Ingredients

  • 350g (12 oz) linguine or spaghetti
  • 6 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  • 1 1/2 cups cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil + 1 tbsp butter
  • 1/4 tsp crushed red pepper flakes
  • 1/2 cup grated Pecorino Romano or Parmesan
  • 1/2 cup fresh basil leaves, torn

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the pasta until al dente; reserve 1/2 cup pasta water before draining.
  2. Warm olive oil in a large skillet over medium-low heat and cook sliced garlic for 2 minutes until fragrant without browning.
  3. Add halved cherry tomatoes and red pepper flakes; cook 4 to 5 minutes until tomatoes soften and release juices.
  4. Transfer drained pasta into the skillet with butter, a splash of pasta water, and grated cheese, tossing until glossy.
  5. Stir in fresh torn basil off the heat and serve right away with extra cheese.
Chef's Note: Tossing the pasta with a splash of starchy cooking water helps the oil and cheese bind into a velvety coat.
