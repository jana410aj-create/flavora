Creamy Wild Mushroom Risotto

Slow-stirred Arborio rice infused with roasted cremini and shiitake mushrooms, thyme, and aged Parmesan.

Prep Time: 15 mins Cook Time: 28 mins Yield: 4 servings

Ingredients

  • 5 cups (1.2L) warm vegetable or chicken stock
  • 3 tbsp olive oil + 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 350g (12 oz) mixed mushrooms (cremini & shiitake), sliced
  • 1 small shallot, finely minced
  • 1 1/2 cups (300g) Arborio or Carnaroli rice
  • 1/2 cup (120ml) dry white wine or extra warm stock
  • 2/3 cup (65g) grated Parmesan cheese + fresh thyme leaves

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Sear sliced mushrooms in 1 tbsp olive oil over high heat until browned and crisp-edged; set aside.
  2. In a heavy saucepan, melt butter with remaining oil and cook the shallot for 2 minutes until translucent.
  3. Stir in the Arborio rice and toast for 2 minutes until edges look slightly translucent, then add wine/stock and stir until absorbed.
  4. Add warm stock one ladle at a time, stirring frequently and waiting until each addition is mostly absorbed before adding the next.
  5. After about 20–22 minutes, when rice is al dente and creamy, stir in mushrooms, Parmesan, and thyme before serving.
Chef's Note: Warming the stock in a separate pot keeps the cooking temperature steady so the rice releases starch evenly.
